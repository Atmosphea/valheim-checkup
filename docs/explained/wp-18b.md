# WP-18B · Board, table and Details, in plain words

**Bars:** each collection bar now has a row of small notches cut into the steel from where its gold fill ends to the far end, so you see what is left. The notches stop around the engraved name.
**Row height:** when you let go of the slider, the rows burn away, come back at the new size with bigger (or smaller) text and icons, and burn back in.
**Stamps:** all four use the same circle layout as Completed. Missing is the mirror image, with its filled circles at the end. Undiscovered and Unverified fill from the last circle backwards.
**Opening Details:** the letter appears out of nothing in the middle of the screen and unfolds. Nothing comes out of the button any more.
**Closing Details:** the letter browns and darkens, then falls and turns as if blown by wind. A black charred edge races ahead of the glowing ember, and black scraps of paper float away. You hear a crackle.
**Item icon:** much bigger, in a square frame.
**Gourmet:** the food's name sits in the middle, with its picture on the left and the cooking station's picture on the right, plus the station level as a big number. Below that is the recipe: each ingredient's picture, how many, and its name. It comes from the Valheim Wiki (credited). Raw foods show only what exists.
