# WP-15 · Packaging, in plain words

**What was done:** no new features. Everything was put into one zip file, `valheim-checkup-3d.zip`, that you can unzip and use straight away. The version number is now 2.0.0.

**What's in the zip:** the app as one file (`valheim-checkup.html`, double-click to open), the same app as separate files for a website later (`site/`), the full source (`source/`), these explanations, the licence texts, the final check report, the questions for you, and `START-HERE.md`, which explains it all.

**What's left out, on purpose:** your save file, anything exported from it, the build spec and the reference picture (the spec names your character), and the downloaded tools (`npm install` fetches them again). A search of every file in the zip, including inside pictures and fonts, finds your character's name zero times.

**Checks:** every test (parity, golden numbers, censuses) ran on the flat page and on the desk, and on the flat page again at tablet and phone size, with reduced motion and with 3D switched off. The unzipped app was opened from the zip and checked against your save, and the website copy was checked from a small local web server. The results are in `checker-reports/final-audit.md`.

**No version history:** `source/` has no record of earlier versions, because git (the tool that keeps one) isn't installed on your Mac.
