# WP-13 · The flat page, phones and touch, in plain words

**What was built:** when the desk can't run (a phone, a tablet, no WebGL, reduced motion, or `?mode=flat`), the original page layout now wears the desk's parchment and ink instead of the dark theme. It also gets the desk's paper surfaces: Details and the How-to letter unfold, the menus are parchment lists, toasts are paper slips, and the buttons are gold coins that hop and spin. Nothing moves to a new place. On a phone the header keeps the brand whole and the 381 px rule still holds.

**Files:** `src/flat/index.js` (switches the skin on and wires the paper, coins, drop, touch and the help switch), `src/flat/paper.js` (paints the paper texture, a *tile*: a small square image repeated without visible seams), `src/styles/flat.css` (the parchment look), `src/config/render.js` (`flat` block: every flat-mode value).

**How it works:** the page decides desk or flat once, at load. In flat mode it adds the class `vc-paper`, paints a 384 px paper tile in idle time and draws the same ink colours, grain and rough edges as the desk. A file dropped anywhere on the page, even during the tour, loads like the Load button. On touch screens a long press (half a second) on a row opens Details, and tapping a filter opens the parchment list. If your computer could run the desk but you chose reduced motion, the help note shows "Show the 3D desk anyway".

**Try this:** open `dist/valheim-checkup.html?mode=flat` and load a save. Drag a .fch file onto any part of the page. Then make the window 390 px wide, or use the phone view in your browser's developer tools, and press and hold a row.
