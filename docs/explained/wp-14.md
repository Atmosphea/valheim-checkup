# WP-14 · Speed and accessibility, in plain words

**What was built:** no new features. The app was measured against its budgets and fixed where it missed. It was also checked for keyboard and screen-reader use. A *long task* is anything that blocks the page for more than 100 ms, so a click feels stuck. A *draw call* is one "paint this object" order to the graphics card.

**What was fixed:** the first click no longer starts the sound system (it now starts quietly after load). Sounds are prepared in spare moments. The big paper images are passed to the page's styles as short links instead of 400 KB of text that was re-read for every element. The gauntlet's hidden parts are prepared at load, so opening Details no longer stalls. The light-on-paper reading no longer waits for the graphics card. A view change no longer measures the page twice, and its ink wash no longer stutters: the stamp's grain pictures are drawn ahead of time. The glow layer draws fewer objects, and the desk planks are one object instead of many. On High the glow is drawn at half size, which keeps graphics memory under 300 MB. Some inks were too pale to read easily on the paper (*contrast*: how much darker the ink is than the paper behind it), so the status words and soft notes are a little darker and the stamp cards paler.

**Files:** `src/perf.js` (the `?perf` panel), `src/util/image-url.js`, `src/a11y.js`, plus small changes in the audio engine, stage, glow, ink and board code. `tools/wp14-check.cjs` runs every check.

**Try this:** open `dist/valheim-checkup.html?perf`. A small panel at the top left shows frames per second, quality tier, draw calls, JavaScript time, long tasks, memory and start-up time. A number over budget turns red. Click Copy and paste the text into a message.
