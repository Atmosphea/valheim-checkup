# WP-17B · Woodblock, gauntlet, sound and title, in plain words

**Sliding a bar** now fades the page out as the knob travels. Let go early and it comes back; let it click in and the new page washes in.
**Progress** is shown as gold along each bar itself. The bars are thicker, the counter plaque is bigger, and the counts on the tabs are cut deeper and filled dark.
**Burned labels** on the woodblock glow like embers when the gauntlet comes close.
**Row height:** a new up-down slider at the left of the woodblock makes table rows taller or shorter. Rows start a bit taller than before, and your choice is remembered in this browser.
**The gauntlet** curls its fingers over the whole bar, not just the knob. It is now your left hand, reaching in from the bottom left.
**Wood burning:** click and drag on the desk or the woodblock's border to burn a line. It glows, cools to black and stays there next time (only in this browser).
**Sound:** a quiet tavern murmur with the odd laugh plays once you have clicked or pressed a key. The Sound coin turns it off.
**The title** has far more ornate flourishes. Two fancier Gothic fonts were tried, but their capital V reads like a B or D, so the title keeps its font. The report shows all three.
**Speed:** letting go of a slider no longer stutters on normal collections; the very long Carpenter list still has one short hitch from the original app's own code.
