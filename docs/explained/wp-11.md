# WP-11 in plain words: surfaces and popups

- **Details** now unfolds out of the Details button like a folded letter: five quick folds, then it floats above the desk and bobs a little. Close it with ×, Escape or a click outside, and it folds back into its button with a puff of ink. Keyboard users stay inside it until they close it, then land back on the button.
- **How-to** opens the same way. **The help note** is a sealed letter: opening it cracks the wax seal and the letter floats up to be read; the page around it stays usable, and a click elsewhere puts it away.
- **Scrolling a long list** lifts the header (title, stamps, filters) off the table like a slip of paper, with a shadow that grows. The coins it covers can't be clicked until it comes down; Tab to a coin or press Home and it drops back.
- **The title** gets ink flourishes that draw, rest and redraw on their own, never over the letters. Your name writes itself in when a save loads.
- **Favourites** are paper tags on brass pins that sway, swing when air or a thump reaches them, drop in when added and blow away when removed.
- **Menus** for filters and sort are parchment lists (mouse or keyboard). The record mode is a small brass lever.
- **Messages:** the toast is a paper slip at the bottom right; tooltips are little paper slips; errors get a red ink blot; changing numbers wipe in quickly; dropping a save leaves an ink ripple.
- **The tour** dims the lamp and moves a pool of light onto each thing it explains: the Load coin, the board of collections, then the stamps. If a step has nothing to point at (no stamps on Highlights), the light fades out rather than pointing at the wrong thing.
- The flat page is unchanged. Files: `src/surfaces/` (one file per surface, `surfaces.css`), small hooks in `src/tutorial/`, `tools/wp11-check.cjs` (self-check), `unit/wp11.test.mjs`.
