# WP-10 in plain words: ink wash, stamps and dots

- When you choose a collection, the old page washes away to the right like wet watercolour, and the new one soaks in from the left a moment later, line by line. On the desk the paper looks briefly damp behind each wash edge.
- The four count cards (Completed, Missing, Unverified, Undiscovered) are now inked stamps with ornate frames. From about 1 second they thump down one after another, and the desk, board and coins react a little. Each landing leaves a different speckle of stray letters in the ink, so no two stampings look the same.
- The numbers then count up to their value. The ink looks wet and shiny at first and dries over a second or two.
- Under each stamp a grid of dots (10 per row) inks in one by one; a part-full dot is always at least a quarter full. Everything is finished within 3.5 seconds, and the last dot gives a small glint.
- If a number changes later (say you switch the record mode), it wipes to the new value and only the changed dots tick; nothing re-stamps.
- Screen readers and keyboard users get the new page instantly; the washing-out copy is invisible to them. With "reduce motion" switched on you get a quick fade and one soft stamp instead.
- Click two collections quickly and you simply end up on the second one.
- The flat page gets the same wash, stamps and dots, without the desk effects.
- Also: the bar names on the board are now bolder and darker, so they read at normal zoom, and a bug that could stretch the gauntlet's arm into long rods across the desk is fixed.
- Files: `src/ink/` (index.js runs it; ghost.js, masks.js, stamps.js, ink.css), `src/app.js` line 57 (hooks), `tools/wp10-check.cjs` (self-check).
