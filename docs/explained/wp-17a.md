# WP-17A · Camera, layout, smoothness and bugs, in plain words

**The drop-down lists** now open on top of everything and are exactly as wide as their box.
**The camera** no longer zooms with the wheel or a pinch; the wheel just scrolls. It shows the main page by default and moves back to show the whole desk while your pointer is over the woodblock or the coins at the top. Click outside the page (on its margin or the desk) to zoom in a little; click again, or go back onto the page, to zoom out.
**The page is bigger**, and the paper starts closer to it on the left.
**The title, your name line and lifetime records** now belong to the page and scroll with the table. The coins stay where they are, and the old line between header and page is gone.
**Scrolling** is one smooth movement. Rows fade out under the burned top edge and slide under the table's heading.
**Loading** shows the desk and the paper straight away: no grey screen.
**The ink wash** is rougher and quicker and runs over the title too. The stamps land halfway through it.
**Stamps:** Undiscovered and Unverified swapped places, and Missing dots fill from the bottom.
**Smoothness:** no dropped frames while the gauntlet moves, and less of a stutter when you let go of a slider.
**The slider knobs** no longer show a line in their shine, and their flash is softer. **The desk** is darker.
