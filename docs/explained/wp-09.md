# WP-09 in plain words: the gauntlet and the spark are your pointer

- On the desk the normal mouse arrow is hidden. Over the board the steel hand is your pointer: its index fingertip sits where your mouse is, about 4 cm above the board.
- It moves with a little weight and lean, but never falls more than 1.2 cm behind, so it always feels true. Your real mouse position still decides what you hover and click.
- Near a knob it points; right over a knob it opens its thumb; press and it pinches the knob and rides it while you drag the bar.
- Move off the board and the hand stops at the edge as if against a soft wall. Its fingertip cap flips open and a glowing spark leaps out onto your pointer (about a quarter of a second). Come back and the spark flies home and the cap closes. Change your mind halfway and it simply turns around.
- The spark is a tiny warm ember with a halo. It leaves a short trail, throws a few embers forward when you stop sharply, makes a little ring when you click, grows over buttons, turns into a thin ember line over text boxes, and crackles now and then. It lights up the paper, the board and the coins, and coin runes glow when it comes near.
- Leave the hand alone for a few seconds and every 25–40 seconds it stretches its fingers.
- On a touch screen there is no spark; drag a knob with your finger and the hand glides in and pinches it.
- Prefer your normal arrow too? Help → "Also show the normal pointer" (remembered on this browser).
- Files: `src/gauntlet/controller.js` (the behaviour), `src/gauntlet/spark.js` (the spark), `src/gauntlet/cursor.css`.
