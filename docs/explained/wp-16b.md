# WP-16B · The woodblock, its knobs, the gauntlet and the spark, in plain words

**The woodblock** is narrower and much taller now. It runs down off the bottom of the screen and a little off the left side, sitting on top of the paper like a separate block. The slots are further apart, so the steel bars are thicker, longer and their names bigger. The help note moved right, next to the block.

**The bars** rest on the left. Slide one to the right to choose it. While you drag it, the bar that was chosen slides back left by exactly the same amount. Only one bar is ever out.

**The knobs** are round, turned steel pulls with a ridged, knurled grip. **"Achievement collections", "Your Viking" and the note at the bottom** are burned into the wood: black, charred, sunk in, with scorched brown edges. **"Collection targets"** is a big brass counter at the top.

**The gauntlet** has a small white dot under its fingertip (the spark's size) showing where you'll grab or press. The hand hovers just above it. When the spark leaves, the hand stays put. When the spark comes back, the hand swings over to it, turns to meet it, they bump, and the dot pops in. The spark now leaves on one, two or three loops (random), with a burst of sparks that fly out fast and then hang, like slow-motion gunfire.
