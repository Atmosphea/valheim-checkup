# WP-18A · Loading, smoothness, camera and layout, in plain words

**Loading** no longer shows a dark square behind each piece as it inks in (a "damp paper" shading drew it), and the pieces now flow one into the next without the pause after the title, finishing about half a second sooner.
**Smoothness:** the frame drops when the cursor left the table had two causes. The hidden gauntlet's gentle bob made the desk redraw all its shadows every frame, and the camera kept creeping for over two seconds in steps too small to see. Both are fixed.
**Sound:** scrolling the table no longer makes a sound when it stops.
**The gauntlet** rests while the spark is out: it ignores the mouse and now and then stretches. Catching the spark works as before.
**Camera:** the main view is 20 % closer, the woodblock view 50 % closer; the header view zooms out by the same step as before.
**Layout:** the sheet's left edge moved in, so the collection title sits in the middle with equal paper either side, and "Valheim Checkup" with its stats is centred above it.
**Coins** sit in a tighter, uneven cluster clear of any text, and jump and spin harder on hover. A new hover restarts the move at once, and the edge of a coin now counts.
**Load:** the Load coin's words are bigger (in flat mode too). Import stays.
**"start here"** is written in red script around the help coin with a big arrow, until a save is loaded.
