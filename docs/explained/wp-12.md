# WP-12 · Sound, in plain words

**What was built:** the app can now make sounds. Each sound is computed by code (no audio files): a "recipe" adds up sine waves and filtered noise. A *filter* keeps only some pitches, like a tone knob. You cut the list to 21 key sounds, such as the bar clink, coin landing, stamps, dots, spark, ink wash, paper, toast, load and stars. The other 48 are listed but marked `"deferred"` and stay silent.

**Files:** `src/audio/blocks.js` (the sound maths), `src/audio/recipes/*.js` (one function per sound), `src/audio/engine.js` (plays them), `src/audio/wiring.js` (which event plays which sound), `src/audio/soundboard.js` (the mix page), `src/config/audio-mix.json` (every volume and setting).

**How it works:** parts of the app announce *events* such as "a stamp landed". The wiring hears each event and asks the engine to play the matching sound. It plays a little left or right depending on where the object is on screen. All sounds pass through a *compressor* (it evens out loud and quiet) and a *limiter* (a ceiling that keeps the total volume below a set level). Browsers only allow sound after you click or press a key, so the app stays silent until then. The desk features that will send most of these events come in later WPs; today you can already hear load, failed load, stars and toasts.

**Try this:** open `dist/valheim-checkup.html?soundboard`. Press "Coin hop" or "View change". Then drag the gain slider of `bar.latch`, press Play again, and click "Download audio-mix.json". Put that file into `src/config/`, run `npm run build:single`, and your new volume is built in.
