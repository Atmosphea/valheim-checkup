# WP-06 in plain words: the board

- The left side of the desk is now a real wooden board, like the reference picture, but with a plain bevelled border (no carved braid, as you decided).
- It has 13 steel bars, one per view, in the same order as the original side menu, with the same names carved in. Their counts sit on small brass tabs, and a brass strip in each slot fills with your progress.
- To change view you drag a bar's knob to the left. Let go past 60% and it clicks in and the page switches; let go before that and it slides back. Only one bar is ever pulled out.
- A plain click on a bar doesn't switch views (it wiggles). Click twice and a little note says "Drag the knob to the left".
- Keyboard users: Tab to the bars, use the arrow keys, then press Enter or Space and the bar slides out by itself.
- At the top, a brass plaque shows "Collection targets" and your total on rolling number wheels.
- Five gold coins: How to, Load character, Import, Export, and a new Sound coin that mutes the sounds. They hop when you hover them and flip when used.
- Behind the scenes the original buttons are still there, just invisible and sitting exactly over the 3D objects, so everything the old app did still works.
- All the words come from the original app, word for word.
- Moving the mouse quickly makes the paper's edges lift a little, as if there's a breeze.
