# WP-17C · Table, highlights and Details, in plain words

**The table** now lists every item by default, with the missing ones at the top and the completed ones at the bottom in green with white writing. The old "Remaining" choice and all the others are still in the list.
**Highlights** read like "993 Wood Floor 2x2 Built": the big number first, the place (1, 2, 3 …) on the right.
**Details** is smaller and tidier: a row of small tools under the name (wiki, use save record, mark completed, mark missing, add count), the record table and your note side by side, and a short footer that says in plain words what "Completed" means.
**Trophies** show their drop chance in big numbers and what they are used for, taken from the Valheim Wiki (credited; nothing is downloaded).
**Closing Details** burns the letter away in about a second, with glowing edges and floating ash; the darkening behind it simply fades.
**The wiki link** just opens the wiki in a new tab; the letter stays open.
**The letter's text** no longer wobbles against the paper as it floats, and every item has its own faint stains, the same each time.
**Wood burning** no longer leaves a dot when you only click the desk to zoom; you have to drag.
**Version 2.2.0**: everything from WP-17 is in the new zip.
