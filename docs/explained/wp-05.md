# WP-05 · Textures and materials, in plain language

**What was built.** Every surface on the desk now has a proper look: fibrous paper with stains, folds and torn edges; walnut grain on the desk and the woodblock; brushed steel, knurled knobs, hammered gold, brass, leather and rivets; a polished gauntlet that reflects the warm paper. Nothing is a picture file: when the page opens, the graphics card *paints* every texture from maths (noise) in about a third of a second, while the desk fades up from candle-dark. The same "seed" number always paints exactly the same textures.

**The engravings** (bar names, coin legends, the side note, plaque digits) are written in the real fonts, turned into a tiny height map and pressed into the metal. Type `__vc.engraved()` in the console to see every word.

**Skipped, as you asked:** the braided knotwork on the woodblock border. It has a plain bevelled frame instead.

**One thing to try.** Open `dist/valheim-checkup.html?materials` for the review page: twelve cards, one per material, each on four slowly turning shapes. Add `=gold` (or any card name, e.g. `?materials=brushedSteel`) to look at one close up.

**One trade-off for you.** On a Retina screen the High level now draws at normal sharpness instead of double, to stay under the 300 MB graphics-memory limit (report, open point 1).
