# WP-16C · Header, letters, menus, titles and the table, in plain words

**The ink wash** you see when you open a collection now moves slowly and evenly across the heading, so you can actually see it.
**The header** is smaller. "Valheim Checkup" can't be selected by accident any more. The big "Bring your Viking's progress / Choose .fch" box is gone: use the Load coin, or drop a save anywhere on the page.
**"How progress is read"** is now at the top right, under the coins. **The coins** each sit at their own height with a slight tilt, like coins dropped on a desk.
**The help letter** unfolds strip by strip, and each strip already shows its part of the letter. It folds back up the same way.
**Details** opens in one quick fold. The Details button peels off the paper and floats while it is open, then settles back.
**The drop-down lists** (status, discovery, biome, sort) open on the paper itself on the desk and move with it.
**Collection titles** are bigger and centred, with drawn flourishes and a red first letter.
**Table columns** can be made wider or narrower by dragging the line between two headings. Your browser remembers the widths. Double-click the line to reset.
**Starred rows** have a slight outline.
The flat page (`?mode=flat`) gets all of these where they fit. This is version 2.1.0.
