# WP-18C · Bosses, records and the 2.3.0 close-out, in plain words

**Bosses:** Highlights now opens with a big square tile for each boss: the seven bosses and Hildir's three. Each shows its trophy picture, its name and how many times you've beaten it. A boss you haven't beaten is a dim, dashed tile.
**Totals:** below the tiles are your lifetime boss kills, last hits, solo kills and kills with others. These count the seven main bosses only. One quiet line says what the save doesn't keep: damage per boss, and how hard the world was for each kill.
**Played on:** the world settings you played under, for example "Combat: hard · Resources: much more · Enemy damage 150 %". They are read from a part of the save the original reader skips, without changing anything it reads. The line appears after you load your save, until you close the tab.
**What the save didn't prove:** the save has two more per-boss numbers. One looks like "last hits", but it doesn't add up to the game's own total for all enemies, so neither is shown.
**Viking records:** about 90 lifetime numbers that were never shown before, in five groups: Combat, Travel & world, Building, Harvest & crafting, and Deaths & oddities. Each has a big number and a plain label, for example jumps, portals, Raven appearances, pieces built and removed, and deaths by falling trees.
**Close-out:** the full test suites ran on the flat page and the desk, the version is 2.3.0, and the zip is rebuilt. Your open questions are in `QUESTIONS-FOR-PAUL.md` (19–28).
**Try it:** load your save and open Highlights.
